# Spooned OSRS 1.0.0

<a href="https://spoonedosrs.com"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chris-fweather/SpoonedOSRS/main/.github/assets/banner.jpg" alt="Spooned OSRS" width="100%"></a>

The first full release · 13 November 2026

Spooned OSRS leaves beta. Our biggest update yet: the first published guide, a World map with directions drawn from the game itself, pet odds checked against the OSRS Wiki for every pet (with per-raid odds and the new Orion), refreshes that wait for no one, hiscores rank titles, max cape versions and a fresh look for legendary titles and emblems. Thank you to everyone who tested the betas.

## The first published guide

**[Tree runs: the basic daily farming run](https://spoonedosrs.com/guides?guide=tree-runs)** by **Obifloat** is the first guide published on Spooned OSRS. It covers the five free tree patches and four fruit tree patches in one daily run, with teleports anyone can use, a map for every stop, what to plant at your level and your odds at Tangleroot. Thank you, Obifloat.

Guides now have thumbs up, beside the guide's title: give one when signed in (click again to take it back). Thumbs up on a player's guides add to their respect on their profile.

## World map

A new Map page in the navigation, in the game's own look:

- pan and zoom with the game's map icons and area names, on map tiles drawn by Spooned OSRS straight from the game's own data;
- switch between the game's own maps, as in game: Gielinor Surface, Misthalin Underground and the other underground and island maps, picked from the list at the top right, and go underground through cave entrances, trapdoors and ladders;
- search towns, dungeons, quests, banks, fairy ring codes and teleports, and turn on layers for quest starts, transport, teleports and banks;
- click any icon for its card, with quest difficulty, length and where it begins;
- get directions through up to eight stops, with walking, shortcuts, boats, fairy rings, spirit trees and the teleports you choose under Teleports & travel.

## Refreshing and searching

- Your stats appear straight away. RuneProfile, TempleOSRS and, when the Hiscores have no answer, Wise Old Man follow in the background. The profile shows "Sync queued", then "Syncing…" with a spinner, and fills in as each source finishes. A busy source is retried by itself, so you no longer have to try again.
- When the official Hiscores answer, a refresh no longer also asks Wise Old Man for the same skills. Connections shows a source that wasn't needed as skipped.
- When the Hiscores no longer list a name with saved progress, the profile says the name may have changed or be hidden, and when its progress was saved, instead of "Player not found".
- Maps keep loading when the tile source is slow or down, and character models are cached by your browser.

## Titles, emblems and crowns

- **Rank titles:** official hiscores ranks earn four tiers for every boss, activity and skill: Champion (#1), Grandmaster (#2–100), Expert (top 500) and Elite (top 1,000). Your chosen rank title follows your rank as it changes.
- **Sir and Squire:** Squire is a new name prefix, the rank below Sir, for helpers and guide writers, shown in bronze beside the gold Sir. Moderators and above keep Sir, and may choose to show Squire instead under Profile settings.
- **Legendary look:** every legendary title, including each Champion title, shares the shifting prismatic look of the Beastmaster and the Completionist. Long titles fit on one line in Profile settings.
- **Emblems:** collection log emblems use the sharper collection log book, a new legendary "All combat tasks" emblem unlocks once every combat achievement task is done, and every legendary emblem shares the Max cape's golden shine and twinkling stars.
- **Leaderboards:** the top three's gold, silver and bronze crowns lead the row, centred on the name and title. Boards open at 20 players per page; choose 10, 15, 20, 25, 35 or 40. Hover or tap "Not synced" to see which sources had data for that player.
- **"the Generous":** now comes with any donation, in any amount or currency, or a supporter grant. Choose whether it shows under Profile settings → Titles and emblems.
- **Respect:** you can give yourself a +1 once on each name linked to your Discord account.

## Character

- **Capes:** the cape slot offers the skill cape of every skill you have at 99, the trimmed cape once you have two or more 99s, and the max cape when every skill is 99.
- **Max cape versions:** Fire, Infernal, Assembler, Masori assembler, Dizana's, Ardougne, Mythical, the imbued and plain god capes and Accumulator, each unlocked by what makes it in game (the cape in your collection log, the Ardougne Elite diary, or the quest).
- **Account menu:** each account's head wears its equipped helmet, hat or mask.

## Pets

Every pet's odds and hours were checked against the OSRS Wiki.

- **Every pet has an expected time,** so luck and progress count all 72. Eleven pets that had none now do, among them the Olmlet, Lil' Zik, Jal-Nib-Rek, Little Nightmare, Dom, Soup and Mr McGroot.
- **Orion,** the pet from The Fractured Archive (1 in 750 a completion), is in the pet journal, and the collection log has The Fractured Archive page: 1,734 unique items.
- **Raid pets work per raid.** The Olmlet follows your points, because it needs a purple; Tumeken's guardian follows your points and raid level; Lil' Zik is 1 in 650 a completion, or 1 in 500 in Hard Mode. Change your points and raid level in the pet window.
- **Phoenix** uses the normal 1 in 5,000 (about 100 hours). Every skilling pet, and Quetzin and Mr McGroot, gets its 200 million XP method (15 times the chance) once your hiscores show 200M in that skill.
- **More methods:** Little Nightmare follows your team size and Dom how deep you delve (both editable in the pet window). Smol Heredit is 1 in 200 a completion, with the quiver exchange as its own method, and Jal-Nib-Rek has on-task and cape-exchange methods. Awakened DT2 kills and Yama contracts are counted on their own.
- **Fixes:** Beaver, Rock golem, Rift guardian, Heron and Rocky have the right rate and pace on each method, so a hiscores sync no longer moves their hours. Tangleroot's expected time uses palm trees (about 82 hours). Callisto, Venenatis and Vet'ion use their own kill paces. Spindel, Artio, Calvar'ion, Gauntlet and master clue counts fill from the hiscores.
- Rates and paces you set yourself are always kept.
- Every pet with a drop rate has a graph, including Rift guardian, and every driest record shows its likelihood.
- An obtained pet without a recorded count is measured by the method you chose, or else the one with a count, and the graph names it. Skilling methods counted in XP use your lifetime XP, so Rocky and other skilling pets show your chance.
- **Collection log emblems** count shares of the full 1,734-item log. An emblem you already show moves to the same share.

## Behind the scenes

- **Sync queue:** runs on Cloudflare Queues (`spoonedosrs-sync`, with a dead-letter queue). One message per source, retries use each source's own wait, a source is never queued twice, and saved progress is never replaced by an empty answer.
- **Wise Old Man pacing:** both WOM lanes share one slot, at 18 requests a minute (90 with an API key). A refusal stops both lanes and backs off: 1, 2, 4 and 8 minutes, capped at 15.
- **Map tiles:** our own, rendered from the OSRS game cache with RuneLite's map renderer by a GitHub Action and served from R2 (`spoonedosrs-map-tiles`), one set per in-game map, with the edge cache in front. A 502 is never cached or stored.
- **Workers Logs:** on for both Workers, with structured lines for syncs, lookups and the queue.
- **Dependencies:** `next` updated to 16.4.6, which clears the open security advisories against earlier 16.x versions. The site is built with vinext and does not run the affected Next.js server code.
- **Build checks:** the build fails if a binding, the queue or logging is missing. Pull request titles must be Conventional Commits.
- **Guides:** guides can be public or staff only. A staff-only guide is invisible to everyone else.
- **Database changes:** migrations `0038` (the tree runs guide), `0039` (provider backoff), `0040` (the "the Generous" choice), `0041` (the sync queue), `0042` (guide visibility and thumbs up), `0043` (collection log emblems out of 1,734), and `0044`–`0047` (the tree runs guide without one paragraph, where it already existed). The minimum migration is `0047_guide_blocks_list_repair.sql`.
