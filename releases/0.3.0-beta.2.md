# Spooned OSRS 0.3.0-beta.2

<a href="https://spoonedosrs.com"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chris-fweather/SpoonedOSRS/main/.github/assets/banner.jpg" alt="Spooned OSRS" width="100%"></a>

Second beta of 0.3 · 4 November 2026

A stability fix. When the site was shared, a rush of visitors and link previews arrived at once and the site briefly stopped answering. This release makes the busiest pages cheap to serve, so a share or a busy evening no longer takes it down.

## For players

- **Sharing the site:** the picture shown when someone shares spoonedosrs.com is now a ready-made image, so link previews load quickly and never slow the site down.
- **Home page:** the leaderboard on the home page is worked out once and shared between visitors, so it loads fast however many people arrive at once. Changes, such as a new sync or a profile made private, still show straight away.
- **Player cards:** a player's shared card is kept for five minutes. If a player makes their profile private, or staff hide it, the very next request for their card gets the plain site image instead, with no player details in it.

## Behind the scenes

- **Site share image:** `public/brand/og-share.jpg` (112 KB, rendered once from the share card) replaces the image drawn on request for the site's link previews. Player pages keep their own card from `/api/share`.
- **Leaderboard:** board answers are kept for up to 60 seconds in Cloudflare's cache, shared by every Worker instance in a location and keyed by the board version, so any change is shown at once. Requests that arrive while a board is being worked out share the one result.
- **Player share cards:** a public profile's card is cached for 5 minutes under the site-wide board version plus the player's own journal revision, so any visibility, rank, badge or presentation change, or a sync, is a new key. A private profile (owner or staff) is answered before the cache is read, with the static `og-share.jpg` (`no-store`, no player data, nothing drawn). `lib/share-cache.ts` holds the key and the rule, with a unit test.
- **No database changes.**
