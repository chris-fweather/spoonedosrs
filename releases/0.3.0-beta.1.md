## For players

- **Guide maps:** a guide can show the game world with numbered stops. Each stop can say:
  - what to do there;
  - who to talk to and which chat options to pick;
  - which items to use.

  The route between stops follows the real walking path, and teleports show as dashed jumps. Teleport stops list every way to get there. Each map is a wide section of its own: the steps on the left, the map on the right (under the map on phones).
- **Hover cards for game terms:**
  - items show Grand Exchange prices, alchemy values and the stack's worth, and long names fit;
  - potions say what they do; diary rewards and untradeable quest items say where they come from;
  - skills show what they do and key levels;
  - prayers and spells show their level and unlocks, and spells their spellbook and runes;
  - teleports, fairy rings and spirit trees show what they need and a small map of where you land.
- **Guide layout:**
  - a contents list beside long guides;
  - two-column sections;
  - gear and inventory panels with a tab per setup;
  - inventories with stacks and noted items, valued in full;
  - cost tables per run or per spell cast, with a spell's runes filled in;
  - requirements as plain rows with bigger icons and the amount beside each item, plus recommended levels and items.
- **Reward windows:** quest scrolls, diary scrolls and chest loot drawn in the game's own style.
- **Drop tables:** the pet and other rare collection log drops are marked in gold. Groups fold open with a proper arrow.
- **Guides list:** filter by the game's own groups (Bosses, Raids, Clues, Minigames, Quests, diaries and more), with bigger cards.
- **Pet luck:** the profile Overview shows your pet summary, worked out like PetCord's pet hours sheet: overall luck in hours, pets collected, total hours and hours left.
  - luckiest and unluckiest pets join Driest and Quickest hunts;
  - pet hovers show expected hours and your chance of having the pet by now;
  - each pet's methods sit in one list, each with its own count;
  - pets that appear after a sync are dated with your kill count at the time.
- **Player timeline:** the Collection timeline now covers pets, the collection log and skill milestones, each switched on and off.
- **Skilling pets:** each has its own drop table, with your chance at your level for every fish, rock, tree or course.
- **Quests and diaries:** requirements use the same compact tiles as guides, with price cards on items. "Read our guide" shows when a guide covers them.
- **Boards:** slimmer rows, top 10 on the home page, and names that line up, with Sir beside the name.
- **Supporters:**
  - a donation supports the profile it was given to;
  - your total counts everything you've given, and you appear once on the supporters list under the name you choose;
  - claim an anonymous donation with its one-time code.
- **Account settings:** tabs for Account, Profiles and Supporter, plus notices about your profiles (such as a link review and its outcome).
- **Credits:** a new page thanking the OSRS Wiki, RuneProfile, TempleOSRS, Wise Old Man, PetCord and the map and travel data, plus guide writers, the staff team and a Hall of fame. Anyone listed can hide themselves.
- **RuneLite plugin page:** what the Spooned OSRS plugin will sync and how to install it, marked as coming soon to the RuneLite Plugin Hub.
- **Link previews:** shared links show the castle scene with your name, scimitar and title.
- **Sign-in:** your account shows straight away when you change pages, without a "Sign in" flash.
- **Character:** scenes are switched off while they're redone; your saved choices are kept.

## For staff

- **Hover titles:** each staff member's scimitar card shows a job title (such as Head Admin), then their Rank and Name prefix. Set it in the Team list; changes are logged in Access history.
- **Latest activity:** reads as plain sentences and includes published guides.
- **Guide editor:**
  - map stops with their own text;
  - inventory stacks and noted items;
  - reward windows;
  - spells in cost tables;
  - two-column sections;
  - tabs on gear and inventory sections.

## Behind the scenes

- **Migrations:** seven new migrations, applied automatically on deploy.
  - `0031_supporter_people.sql` counts supporters per person.
  - `0032_plugin_sync.sql` adds plugin tokens, seen accounts and profile notifications.
  - `0033_credits_listing.sql` lets people hide themselves from Credits.
  - `0034_profile_donations.sql` records donations per profile and each plugin token's last sync.
  - `0035_donation_claim_hash.sql` claims anonymous donations by code.
  - `0036_plugin_account_owners.sql` keeps a plugin-proven account with its Discord account.
  - `0037_staff_title.sql` adds staff hover titles.
- **Still in development:** the world map page and the plugin's own features (tokens and verified profiles) stay off the live site until they're ready.
- **Running your own copy:** `npm run setup` prepares a fresh clone, and the checks run without any configuration.
