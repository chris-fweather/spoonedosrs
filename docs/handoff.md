- `feat/guide-map-lines` (built on dev): route lines on guide maps. A coloured line per walking leg between consecutive stops on the shown floor (none into a teleport stop or across floors), arrow at the midpoint of long legs, stop badges in the list and on the map share the leg colour, hovering a stop highlights its leg. Per-map `lines?: boolean` (missing = on; only `false` is stored), "Route lines" checkbox in the map editor. Logic: `guideMapLegs`/`guideMapStopColours` in `lib/guide-map.ts`. No migration.
- `feat/guide-map-paths` (built on dev): guide map route lines follow the walking path tile by tile. `walkingPath` in `lib/guide-map-paths.ts` runs A* on the world map collision data plus same-floor doors and shortcuts (`walkLinks`: travel.json `passage`/`shortcut` edges up to 12 tiles), in a box around the two stops, with steps costing 2 straight and 3 diagonal and straight runs collapsed. Stops on blocked tiles snap to the side facing the other stop; a locked-door stop can be left from the next tile; an unreachable stop (jail cell) is reached from the nearest walked tile within 4. No path (water, legs over 320 tiles, a walk over 5x the distance + 40) keeps a straight dashed line. Paths come from the world map worker (`app/world-map-route.worker.ts`, request `paths`) via `app/guide-map-paths.ts`: one worker per page, started only when a map with lines renders, results cached per leg; a leg draws nothing until its path arrives (about 1.5 s for the first map: collision.dat 778 KB, travel.json ~90 KB gzipped, both cached by the browser and shared with `/map`). Stops sharing a tile sit side by side. Readers get a Route on/off button over each map's top right corner (`aria-label="Show route"`, `aria-pressed`); hiding is remembered in `localStorage` `spooned:guide-maps:route` for every map that shows lines by default, and a map whose writer turned lines off can be turned on for that visit only. Tests: `lib/guide-map-paths.test.ts`. No migration.
