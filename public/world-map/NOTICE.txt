World map data notices
======================

The files in this folder are built by scripts/build-world-map.mjs (exits.json by scripts/build-map-exits.mjs) and loaded
by the /map page and guide maps.

1. Shortest Path
----------------
collision.dat, travel.json, travel-options.json, exits.json, and the banks, transport stops and teleport
arrivals in places.json are derived from Shortest Path by Skretzo and contributors
(https://github.com/Skretzo/shortest-path, commit c48960159a738066a230f76899821da9d5369439,
src/main/resources). Changes: the collision map keeps only the two movement bits per tile; transports,
teleports and banks keep only positions, durations and requirements. lib/guide-travel.json (built by
scripts/build-guide-travel.mjs for guide travel hover cards) is derived from travel.json and places.json.

3. RuneLite
-----------
icon-details.json (built by scripts/build-map-icon-details.mjs) takes agility shortcut names, Agility levels and the
world map points of their icons from RuneLite's AgilityShortcut enum, the fish of each kind of fishing spot and the map
squares named in its NPC ids from FishingSpot, and Fishing levels from the skill calculator's FishingAction
(https://github.com/runelite/runelite, commit 9fad2c588ca957ae47d883b09544725a796ed9fe). Changes: only names, levels
and positions are kept, matched to the game's map icons; names are in sentence case. The obstacle names and other
skills of shortcuts RuneLite does not place come from Shortest Path (section 1).

4. Other sources
----------------
Quest names, difficulty, length and start text come from the Old School RuneScape Wiki
(https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/), CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (lib/quest-reference.json; see
public/DATA-SOURCES.txt).

Map tiles are rendered from the game by Mejrs (https://github.com/mejrs/layers_osrs) and served
through /api/map-tile. Old School RuneScape, its world and its game data are (c) Jagex Ltd.

game-icons.json (map-function icon placements and area labels) and the sprites in public/icons/map/game are read
from the game cache (world map geography, world map composite, map element and object configs, sprites); preview
only, see lib/world-map-game-icons.ts.
